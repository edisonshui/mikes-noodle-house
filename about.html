The Founder · 創辦人

From Hong Kong
to Seattle's Heart

從香港到西雅圖唐人街

Mike arrived in Seattle in the early 1980s carrying one thing above all else: a deep love for Hong Kong's noodle-house culture — the steaming broth, the perfectly folded wontons, the clatter of bowls at six in the morning. He had watched his grandmother cook this way, and her mother before her.

Mike於八十年代初抵達西雅圖，心中懷抱著對港式麵家文化的深厚情感——滾燙的湯底、手工包裹的雲吞、清晨六點碗碟碰撞的聲響。那是祖母的手藝，也是曾祖母的傳承。

In 1986, he opened 全記麵家 on Maynard Avenue South, in the middle of Seattle's Chinatown-International District. There were no shortcuts. The broth was slow-simmered from pork bones for hours. The egg noodles were handmade each morning. The wontons were folded by hand, just as they had always been.

1986年，他在西雅圖唐人街美納德大道南開設全記麵家。湯底以豬骨熬煮數小時，蛋麵每日手製，雲吞一個個親手包裹。一切如舊，從不簡化。

Our Craft · 我們的工藝

The Art Behind Every Bowl

每一碗背後的工藝
一
豬骨清湯
Pork Bone Broth

Each morning begins with pork bones, ginger, and time. We simmer our broth for hours until it runs clear and carries a natural sweetness that no shortcut can replicate. The broth is the soul of every bowl.

二
手製雲吞
Hand-Folded Wontons

Our wontons are folded by hand every day — paper-thin wrappers filled with fresh pork and shrimp, sealed with a single practiced fold. Each one is made to order, never pre-made, never frozen.

三
新鮮蛋麵
Fresh Egg Noodles

The noodles arrive fresh each morning — thin, springy, with just the right bite. They are blanched to order and served immediately, so every strand carries the broth without going soft.
