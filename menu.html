麵

Noodle Soups

Served in a light, clear broth with fresh egg noodles or flat rice noodles. Available in regular (小) and large (大). Most soups range from $11–$13.

Rice Congee · 粥類

粥

Rice Congee

Silky smooth rice porridge, slow-cooked until the grains melt into a velvety base. A Cantonese comfort food and a staple of Hong Kong breakfast culture. Served warm.

Classic 魚片豬潤粥 Fish & Liver Congee
Signature 荔灣艇仔粥 Sam-pan Congee
