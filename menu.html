New Here? · 第一次來

Not sure what to order?

Five dishes that represent us well — a good starting point for your first visit.

01
雲吞麵 Wonton Noodle Soup

Our signature. Handmade shrimp-pork wontons in a light, clear broth with springy egg noodles. The dish we're known for — clean, satisfying, and deeply Hong Kong.

Signature bowl · Classic starting point
02
雲吞牛腩麵 Wonton & Beef Brisket Noodle

Wontons paired with slow-braised beef brisket in a richer, deeper broth. All the flavour of the classic, with a heartier bite from the tender brisket.

Hearty · House favourite
03
牛腩撈麵 Beef Brisket Dry Noodle

Tender braised beef brisket over noodles with a rich, savoury sauce — no broth. Comforting and full-flavoured, with every strand coated in the braising sauce.

Rich & saucy · No broth
04
荔灣艇仔粥 Sam-pan Congee

Silky smooth rice porridge (congee) slow-cooked until velvety, topped with seafood and garnishes. A Hong Kong breakfast staple — warm, gentle, and deeply comforting.

Warm & comforting · Great for congee first-timers
05
油條 Youtiao · Fried Dough Stick

Crispy, golden fried dough sticks — light inside, crunchy outside. Add one as a side and break it into your congee. Simple, but a must-try pairing.

Best side · Pairs with congee
Noodle Soups · 麵類

麵

Noodle Soups

Served in light, clear broth with fresh springy egg noodles. Wonton (雲吞) are handmade shrimp-pork dumplings. Sui-kau (水餃) are larger dumplings with a mixed filling. Available in regular (小) and large (大). Most soups $11–$13 — see full menu for current pricing.

Signature 雲吞麵 Wonton Noodle Soup Handmade wontons · clear broth · egg noodles
House Favourite 雲吞牛腩麵 Wonton & Beef Brisket Noodle Wontons with slow-braised brisket · richer broth
Classic 雲吞水餃麵 Wonton & Sui-kau Noodle Two dumpling types together · egg noodles
Rice Congee · 粥類

粥

Rice Congee

Congee (粥) is rice porridge slow-cooked until the grains dissolve into a smooth, velvety base. Mild and deeply comforting — served warm with different toppings depending on the variety. Pairs perfectly with youtiao (fried dough sticks) on the side.

Classic 魚片豬潤粥 Fish & Liver Congee Sliced fish and pork liver over silky congee
Signature 荔灣艇仔粥 Sam-pan Congee Seafood porridge with mixed toppings · house signature
Sides & Drinks · 小菜 飲料

小菜

Sides & Drinks

Youtiao (油條) are long, crispy golden fried dough sticks — light inside, crunchy outside. Break one apart and dip it into warm congee for the classic pairing. We also offer seasonal greens, soy milk, and more. See the full printed menu for all sides and drinks.

Must Try 油條 Fried Dough Stick Crispy golden dough · dip into congee
Seasonal 各式小菜 Daily Sides Seasonal vegetables and small dishes
Drinks 豆漿 Soy Milk & More Warm soy milk and traditional beverages
