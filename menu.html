Rice Congee · 粥類

粥

Rice Congee

Silky smooth rice porridge, slow-cooked until the grains melt into a velvety base. A Cantonese comfort food and a staple of Hong Kong breakfast culture. Served warm.

白粥 from $14
Plain Congee

Pure silky rice porridge, plain and clean. The perfect base — often paired with youtiao or a side dish. Mild, soothing, and deeply comforting.

及第粥 from $14
Mixed Pork Congee

A traditional Hong Kong classic. Pork liver, lean pork, and intestines cooked directly in the congee — earthy, rich, and deeply savory.

牛肉粥 from $14
Beef Congee

Tender slices of fresh beef, cooked just right in hot silky porridge. Garnished with ginger and green onion. Clean and satisfying.

皮蛋瘦肉粥 from $14
Century Egg & Pork Congee

One of the most iconic Cantonese congees. Dark, creamy century egg adds depth and umami to silky porridge with lean pork — a must-try for first-timers.

豬潤粥 from $14
Pork Liver Congee

Thinly sliced pork liver with a silky, just-cooked texture in smooth congee. Savory and slightly rich — a traditional HK breakfast staple.

魚片粥 from $14
Fish Fillet Congee

Fresh fish slices, delicately poached in hot congee. Light and clean-flavored — one of the most popular choices for those new to Cantonese congee.

Sides & Drinks
