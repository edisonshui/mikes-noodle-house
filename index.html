Our Story · 我們的故事

A Bowl Built on Tradition,
A Kitchen Built on Love

一碗麵，一段傳奇

Tucked in the heart of Seattle's Chinatown-International District, Mike's Noodle House — 全記麵家 — has been serving hand-crafted wonton noodle soup and silky congee since 1986. For the Hong Kong families who have called this neighborhood home for decades, it is not just a restaurant. It is where you go when you need to feel like you never left.

自1986年開業至今，全記麵家以正宗港式雲吞麵及粥品為西雅圖華埠服務近四十年。對於在此落地生根的香港移民而言，這裡不只是一間食肆，更是他們心中的「歸處」。

Founder Mike brought his family's craft across the Pacific — hand-folded wontons with paper-thin wrappers, slow-simmered pork bone broth, fresh egg noodles made each morning. A tradition that has never once cut corners. Four decades of the same honest commitment: food that never lies.

Mike將祖傳手藝帶到美國，以現熬豬骨湯、手製蛋麵，每日新鮮，從不妥協。四十年如一日，食物不欺人。
