Our Story · 我們的故事

Forty Years.
The Same Honest Bowl.

Mike's Noodle House — 全記麵家 — has been a fixture of Seattle's Chinatown-International District since 1986. Hand-folded wontons, slow-simmered pork bone broth, fresh egg noodles made each morning. No shortcuts, no changes. For the families who grew up here, it's never been just a restaurant.
